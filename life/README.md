# Photos for the montage

Drop your photos in this folder, then list the filenames in the `LIFE`
array near the bottom of `index.html`.

Any shape works — tiles crop to fill. Roughly 800px on the long edge is
plenty; anything larger just makes the page heavier.

Suggested naming (nothing enforces it, it's just tidy):

    friends-1.jpg  friends-2.jpg
    watching-1.jpg
    food-1.jpg  food-2.jpg  food-3.jpg
    hoops-1.jpg
    fbla-1.jpg
    building-1.jpg

A group with no photos listed still renders — you get a labelled
placeholder tile instead of a broken image.
